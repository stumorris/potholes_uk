Where the data comes from

The map combines reports made here with public data from councils and other services. There's no single national pothole database, so we add sources council by council as they publish open data.

Your council isn't here?

Many councils publish highway defect data on their open data portal or data.gov.uk; others will release it in response to a Freedom of Information request. If you find a dataset, tell us and we'll add it.

Council data is used under the Open Government Licence v3.0 unless stated. Council lookup uses postcodes.io (ONS data, OGL). Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
